# Converge Underwriting brand

Converge Underwriting is the underwriting-intelligence product concept within the
[Converge AI](https://convergeai.co.za/) brand family.

## Brand relationship

The parent company describes itself as an enterprise-grade AI automation consultancy
focused on AI agents, workflow automation, and governed knowledge systems. Converge
Underwriting applies that same proposition to commercial insurance: source evidence,
approved precedent, and human expertise converge into controlled decisions.

## Visual system

- **Parent mark:** `app/static/converge-mark.svg` — the existing Converge AI mark,
  preserved without alteration.
- **Product lockup:** `app/static/converge-underwriting-logo.svg` — the parent mark
  paired with the Converge Underwriting wordmark.
- **PNG exports:** matching transparent `.png` files alongside both SVG assets for
  tools and channels that do not support vector artwork.
- **Primary typeface:** Inter.
- **Primary gradient:** `#0a47f7` to `#6b1ef9`.
- **Ink:** `#0f1120`.
- **Product accent:** `#4f6ef7`.

Use the horizontal lockup for README files, presentations, and wide collateral. Use
the parent mark with a live text label in compact product navigation. Preserve clear
space around the mark and do not recolour, distort, rotate, or add effects to it.
