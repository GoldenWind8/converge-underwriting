# Chat transcript — Golden Crust Bakery, September 2025

**Broker:** Golden Crust Bakery, industrial bakery supplying supermarkets, 22 staff,
trading 15 years. Fire and Business Interruption.

**Underwriter:** Ovens gas or electric?

**Broker:** Two gas deck ovens and a rotary rack oven. They *do* have a current gas
certificate of conformity, renewed in January, and a serviced sprinkler system.

**Underwriter:** Good — certificate on file plus sprinklers keeps the gas side down to
MEDIUM despite the fire load; flour dust in the mixing area is the thing to note, keep
housekeeping records. What about their supply chain?

**Broker:** One supermarket group is about 70% of turnover.

**Underwriter:** That concentration is a real BI exposure — losing that contract or a
halt in supply hits most of the income. HIGH on key-customer dependency; suggest they
look at an extended indemnity period.
