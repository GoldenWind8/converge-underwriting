# Chat transcript — Supreme Auto Body (panel beater), June 2025

**Broker:** New risk: Supreme Auto Body, panel beating and spray painting, 8 staff,
single workshop in Boksburg. Fire, Business Interruption and Public Liability.

**Underwriter:** Spray booth — is it a proper enclosed booth with certified extraction,
or open spraying?

**Broker:** Enclosed booth, but the extraction certificate expired last year. Thinners
and paints stored in a steel cabinet, about 400 litres on site at any time.

**Underwriter:** Expired extraction certification plus 400 litres of flammables — that's
HIGH for me on the fire side. Solvent vapour in a booth with uncertified extraction is a
flash-fire risk, and it voids most warranties. Condition: recertify the booth.

**Broker:** Understood. BI: they're the only premises, no alternative workshop.

**Underwriter:** Single-location dependency, HIGH on BI — a booth fire stops all income.
Recommend they price a 12-month indemnity minimum. Customers on the floor?

**Broker:** Customers drop and collect in a marked reception area only.

**Underwriter:** Fine, that keeps liability LOW.
