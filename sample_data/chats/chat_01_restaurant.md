# Chat transcript — Mama's Kitchen (restaurant), March 2025

**Broker:** Hi, submitting Mama's Kitchen — 60-seater restaurant in Rosebank, 14 staff,
been trading 9 years. They want Fire and Public Liability. Buildings owner-occupied,
brick construction.

**Underwriter:** Thanks. Gas or electric kitchen?

**Broker:** Gas — two commercial ranges plus a gas griddle. LPG cylinders in a cage outside.

**Underwriter:** Do they have a gas installation certificate of conformity? That's the
first thing the insurer will ask for after any kitchen fire.

**Broker:** Checking... no, nothing on file since the kitchen was refitted in 2023.

**Underwriter:** Then I'm rating that HIGH. An uncertified gas installation after a refit
is exactly how fire claims get repudiated. They must get a certified installer to issue
the certificate — I'll note it as a condition. Extraction canopy cleaning contract?

**Broker:** Yes, quarterly with FlameGuard, serviced extinguishers too.

**Underwriter:** Good, that keeps the ducting grease loading down to a medium concern —
frying operations still mean elevated fire load. Public access is obviously constant for
a restaurant, standard slip/trip exposure, low.
